Privacy Policy: Adventures of Cuetip

Last updated: October 5, 2026. Published by CuetipLLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Adventures of Cuetip does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

Your save files, unlocked endings and settings are saved only on your own device so your progress is there next time. Deleting the app (or clearing site data on the web) removes them.

Age rating

The game is intended for adults (18+) because of its mature themes. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, this page will be updated before that version ships.
