## Title
Adventures of Cuetip, Vol. 1: Soul Searching

## Short description / tagline
Cursed, running out of time, and searching for the woman from his dreams. A pixel-art story adventure with training battles.

## Description

Every night, Cuetip dreams of the same woman: impossibly long hair, standing beneath a tree made of starlight, her back always turned.
*"Find me. Before the dark does."*

Then Vexmoor, the Rot King, arrives at the Lantern Festival and curses Cuetip with **HS**, a black disease that spreads across his body, piece by piece, toward his heart. There's no cure. But with his medicine, Cuetip can keep going.

So he goes.

**On the road he meets the friends who make him stronger:**
- **Bramble**, an ancient tortoise monk, teaches him to Guard and Parry
- **Dr. Moss**, a gentle moth healer, gives him the medicine that keeps HS at bay, and teaches him to Focus
- **Kit**, a fox swordswoman who walked out on Vexmoor's army, teaches him Dash Strike
- **Ozzie**, a retired champion bear, teaches him the Heavy Blow and entrusts him with the Starfang Claws

Follow three secret clues to the Black Spire, face Vexmoor, and find her.

**Features**
- A complete story: about 30–45 minutes
- 9 real-time battles with readable, touch-friendly controls: parry, dash through lunges, smash guards
- The HS meter: the curse rises in every fight, and your medicine pushes it back
- Hand-made pixel art and original generated music
- Save/load, auto/skip, history log, and a photosensitive mode
- Plays in your browser on desktop and phones (portrait or landscape)
- Lose a fight? Retry, retry on easier settings, or skip it

**Controls**
Click or tap to read. In battle: J strike · K guard · L dash · I heavy (hold) · U focus · H shot · Esc pause. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

**Content warning (18+):** fantasy violence, a chronic incurable illness with medication and injections, a cruel villain and dark themes, strong language, flashing effects (can be turned off). No sexual content.
